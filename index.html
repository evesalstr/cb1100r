DALSLAND – DAY TRIP GUIDE

A beautiful trip with nature, history, art and great food.

Meet location: The Visitor Stugby, Håverud Show on map →

Highlights & Things to See

Håverud Aqueduct

Built in 1868. A unique place where road, railway and water meet.

  • Boats travel across the rapids in a metal trough
  • Locks, cafés, boat traffic and guided boat tours
  • One of Dalsland's most famous attractions

Not Quite & The Brick, Fengersfors

Old industrial site, now a cultural centre.

  • Former paper mill, now full of life again
  • Over 70 artists and creative businesses
  • Studios, shops, café and bakery
  • A living creative environment – not a traditional museum

Högsbyn Rock Carvings

Over 2,000 carvings from the Bronze Age.

  • One of the largest rock carving sites in the region
  • Walking trail of about 2.5 km
  • Relaxing area and beautiful surroundings
  • A very atmospheric place

Charming Village & Surroundings

Small charming village in scenic nature.

  • Baroque church from the 18th century
  • Flower farm
  • Local countryside shop
  • Close to hiking trails

Red Wooden Church

Red wooden church from the 18th century.

  • Beautifully located by the lake
  • Voted one of Sweden's most beautiful churches
  • Popular wedding location

Herb Garden, Dals Rostock

Around 280 plants (food, medicine, dyeing).

  • Connected to an old spa tradition from the 18th century
  • Café, studio, shop and second-hand bookstore
  • Calm and very beautiful setting

Nature & Activities

Cycling Route

Forests, lakes and countryside.

  • BBQ by the cycling route
  • Relaxing spots
  • Beautiful picnic locations

Lelångsleden (cycling route)

Scenic cycling along lakes and canals.

  • Mix of gravel roads and small roads
  • Suitable for both short rides and longer cycling trips

Food & Coffee

Håverud Brasserie

Located right by the canal.

  • Specialises in smoked fish from their own smokehouse
  • Also serves grilled dishes, pasta, salads and desserts
  • Cosy setting, especially outdoors by the water

Håverud Smokehouse

Locally produced fish delicacies.

  • Both traditional and unique specialities

Ice Cream & Café by the Canal

Perfect summer stop.

  • Ice cream & café by the canal
  • Beautiful canal views

Fengersfors / Not Quite Area

Café and bistro in an old industrial setting.

  • Wood-fired artisan bakery
  • Enjoy a fika in the middle of a creative environment

Dalsland Art Museum Café

Amazing lake view.

  • Peaceful place for a break
  • Local and organic ingredients

Why Go Here?

Dalsland offers a perfect combination of nature, history, culture and hospitality. Ride some of Sweden's most scenic roads, enjoy local food and cafés, and experience the peaceful atmosphere that makes visitors stay longer than they originally planned.

  • Nature and water
  • History (from the Bronze Age to industrial times)
  • Art and creativity
  • Good food and cafés

It's not just a place to visit – it's a place you'll want to stay longer than planned.
